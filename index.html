✦ Neurons firing together strengthen synapses, forming long-term memories.
When you study new concepts, the synapses between your neurons undergo chemical changes. Donald Hebb famously summarized this biological mechanism: "neurons that fire together, wire together," forming the cellular foundation of learning.
✦ Multitasking triggers rapid cognitive switching, draining mental focus.
Cognitive psychology shows that the human brain cannot focus on two complex tasks at once. Instead, it switches back and forth, incurring a "cognitive switch cost" that drains mental energy, increases errors, and slows retention.
✦ Entering flow state accelerates synaptic plasticity and filters distractions.
Flow is a state of deep absorption and concentration where external distractions are naturally filtered out. In this state, the efficiency of neural changes increases due to targeted neurotransmitter release in the brain.
